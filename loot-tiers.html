Loot Tiers

Items found throughout the server are classified into five rarity tiers based on their value, usefulness, and availability.

Common

Frequently encountered items with relatively low value. Common loot can be found throughout most areas of the map.

Uncommon

Less frequently encountered equipment and resources with increased value or utility.

Rare

Valuable equipment and resources generally found in more dangerous or valuable loot locations.

Epic

Extremely valuable items with limited availability. Epic loot is typically associated with dangerous locations and high-value activities.

Legendary

The highest classification of loot available. Legendary items are exceptionally valuable and among the most difficult items to acquire.
