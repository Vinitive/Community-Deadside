Getting Started

Everything you need to know before heading out into the world. This server includes custom loot, modded systems, high-value locations, and progression beyond the base Deadside experience.

Your First Steps

01

Get Equipped

Begin by searching nearby locations for basic weapons, ammunition, medical supplies, food, and equipment.

02

Learn the Loot Tiers

Items are divided into Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, and Legendary tiers. Higher-tier items are generally more valuable and harder to obtain.

View Loot Tiers →
03

Build Your Economy

Sell loot, collect valuable resources, and work toward the equipment needed to participate in the server's more advanced systems.

04

Explore the Map

Valuable loot and modded items are spread throughout the map. Exploring beyond the most common locations can lead to significantly better rewards.

Custom Server Content

Modded Items & Systems

The server includes custom weapons, keycards, equipment, and progression systems that are not part of the standard Deadside experience.

Important Early Goals

01 Establish a reliable source of weapons and ammunition.

Quick Tips

Explore: Some of the server's most valuable items are intentionally spread across the map rather than concentrated in one area.

Keep unusual items: Components that appear unimportant may be required for crafting or custom systems later.

Prepare before high-value locations: Areas containing Epic and Legendary loot should be treated as end-game destinations.

Check the wiki: Modded items and systems are documented here so you can understand what an item does before selling or discarding it.
