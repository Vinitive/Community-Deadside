MODDED SYSTEM

GDM Bitcoin System

Epic

The GDM Bitcoin System introduces a complete Bitcoin mining system to the server.

HOW IT WORKS

Players must explore the map and collect GDM RAM Sticks, Motherboards, Gold GPUs, and CPUs to assemble their mining equipment.

MINING REQUIREMENT

The system requires 1 Gold GPU for every 1 Bitcoin produced, making GPUs a valuable and consumable part of the mining process.

WHY IT MATTERS

The system adds new high-value items to the economy and gives players another reason to explore the full extent of the map while searching for the components needed to keep their mining operation running.

MODDED ITEM

GDM Trader Keycard

Epic

A high-value access card used to gain entry to the GDM Trader located in the southwestern region of the map.

HOW TO OBTAIN

The GDM Trader Keycard must be crafted at the GDM Workbench using a Broken Keycard. Broken Keycards can be found while looting military sites throughout the map.

USE

Opens the secured doors leading into the GDM Trader located in the southwestern part of the map.

CONSUMED ON USE

Yes. The Trader Keycard is consumed when used, meaning players will need to obtain and craft another keycard for future access.

MODDED WEAPON

CheyTac Intervention

Legendary

A devastating long-range sniper rifle chambered in .50 BMG, capable of dropping most enemies with a single well-placed shot.

WEAPON DETAILS

The CheyTac Intervention uses a 5-round magazine and comes equipped with a custom 8x scope designed for long-range engagements.

DAMAGE

The rifle can one-shot kill most enemies, making it one of the most powerful weapons available on the server.

TRADEOFF

Its extreme stopping power is balanced by a slower fire rate, making shot placement and timing important during combat.

WHERE TO FIND

The CheyTac Intervention can be found at the MRF.

AMMUNITION

.50 BMG

MODDED WEAPON

AS Val

Legendary

A highly lethal suppressed rifle built for close to medium-range combat, offering strong damage and fast target elimination.

WEAPON DETAILS

The AS Val uses a 25-round magazine and is chambered in 7.62x39 ammunition.

DAMAGE

Headshots will one-shot most enemies, while body shots provide a very fast time-to-kill.

FIRE MODES

The weapon does not support full-auto fire. It is limited to burst and semi-automatic firing modes.

TRADEOFF

Its powerful damage output is balanced by the lack of full-auto fire and a restrictive 25-round magazine capacity.

ATTACHMENTS

The AS Val can use most base-game weapon attachments, allowing players to customize it for different combat roles.

WHERE TO FIND

The AS Val can be found at the MRF.
