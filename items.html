MODDED SYSTEM

GDM Bitcoin System

Epic

The GDM Bitcoin System introduces a complete Bitcoin mining system to the server.

HOW IT WORKS

Players must explore the map and collect GDM RAM Sticks, Motherboards, Gold GPUs, and CPUs to assemble their mining equipment.

MINING REQUIREMENT

The system requires 1 Gold GPU for every 1 Bitcoin produced, making GPUs a valuable and consumable part of the mining process.

WHY IT MATTERS

The system adds new high-value items to the economy and gives players another reason to explore the full extent of the map while searching for the components needed to keep their mining operation running.

MODDED ITEM

GDM Trader Keycard

Epic

A high-value access card used to gain entry to the GDM Trader located in the southwestern region of the map.

HOW TO OBTAIN

The GDM Trader Keycard must be crafted at the GDM Workbench using a Broken Keycard. Broken Keycards can be found while looting military sites throughout the map.

USE

Opens the secured doors leading into the GDM Trader located in the southwestern part of the map.

CONSUMED ON USE

Yes. The Trader Keycard is consumed when used, meaning players will need to obtain and craft another keycard for future access.
